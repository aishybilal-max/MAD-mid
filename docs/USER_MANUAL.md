# Seaside Tasks — User Manual

1. **Add a task** — tap the glowing **+** button. Enter a title (required), optional description,
   due date and time, category, repeat settings, reminder on/off and optional subtasks, then **Create task**.
2. **Today tab** — pick a day from the week strip. The ring shows today's progress.
   Tap the circle on a card to mark it complete; tap the card to edit it.
3. **Subtasks & progress** — tap a subtask on a card to tick it; the bar and percentage update.
4. **Repeat** — choose Daily, Weekdays (M–S) or Every N days. Completing a repeat task moves it
   to **Done** and schedules the next occurrence automatically.
5. **Delete** — swipe a card to the left (tap **UNDO** in the bar to bring it back) or use the bin icon when editing.
6. **Settings** — Light / Dark / System theme, notification sound (with a test button),
   and export of all tasks as **CSV**, **PDF** or **Email**.
